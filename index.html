Robotics Center of Silicon Valley (YC25)  /  2026

Teleoperated Manipulation

Franka Research 3, dexterous hand, Apple Vision Pro

The challenge

Let an operator drive a 7-DOF arm and a dexterous hand with the motion of their own hand.

What I did

Integrated the teleoperation system end to end. I set up the Apple Vision Pro and the link between it and the NUC computer, brought up ARKit hand tracking, and connected the open source dex-retargeting library so the operator’s hand motion maps onto a 7-DOF arm and a dexterous hand. Then I tuned and debugged the full pipeline in bench and hardware tests.

  • 0.5–1 mmIK positioning error, verified by a forward kinematics check
  • End to endVision Pro to NUC to robot, integrated and debugged as one pipeline on the bench and on hardware
  • No controllerthe operator’s own hand motion drives the arm and the dexterous hand’s fingers
  • Open sourceARKit hand tracking and dex-retargeting brought up and connected on the team’s robot instead of rebuilt from scratch
